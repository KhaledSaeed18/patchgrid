Patchgrid keeps four kinds of record, because restoring a broken service, fulfilling a request, finding a root cause and making a planned change are different jobs. Each type has its own lifecycle, and a ticket can only make the moves its type allows.

## The four record types

| Type            | Number       | What it is for                                              | Who raises it    | SLA |
| --------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --- |
| Incident        | `INC-000042` | Something is broken or degraded now. Restore service fast   | Anyone           | Yes |
| Service Request | `SR-000007`  | A standard request: a laptop, a licence, an account, access | Anyone           | Yes |
| Problem         | `PRB-000003` | The root cause behind one or more incidents                 | Agents and above | No  |
| Change          | `CHG-000011` | A planned modification, approved before it is carried out   | Agents and above | No  |

Agents can also raise a ticket on someone else's behalf. That person is the requester and is told it was raised for them.

Every new ticket's priority is computed from its impact and urgency; see [priority and SLAs](/docs/priority-and-slas). A Free workspace can create 200 tickets a calendar month and a Pro workspace 10,000; see [pricing](/pricing).

## Ticket numbers

Numbers are generated by Patchgrid, counted separately for each type, padded to six digits and never reused. They are per workspace, so your `INC-000001` and another company's `INC-000001` are unrelated.

Search accepts a number in several forms: `INC-000042`, `inc-42`, `#42` or `42`. A bare number matches that number in every type.

## Lifecycles

Status only changes through a named action, such as _resolve_ or _reopen_. Patchgrid offers only the actions that are valid for the ticket's current status and for you; any other move is refused. "Agents" below means agents who can see the ticket (see [roles and permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions)), plus admins and owners.

### Incidents and Service Requests

| From                                 | Action  | To            | Who                                                                        | Requires                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `NEW`                                | assign  | `ASSIGNED`    | Agents                                                                     | —                                                    |
| `NEW`, `ASSIGNED`                    | start   | `IN_PROGRESS` | The assignee, any agent if it is unassigned, admins                        | Starting an unassigned ticket makes you its assignee |
| `IN_PROGRESS`                        | wait    | `PENDING`     | The assignee, admins                                                       | A public comment saying what the requester must do   |
| `PENDING`                            | resume  | `IN_PROGRESS` | The assignee, admins, or automatically when the requester replies publicly | —                                                    |
| `IN_PROGRESS`                        | resolve | `RESOLVED`    | The assignee, admins                                                       | A public resolution note                             |
| `RESOLVED`                           | close   | `CLOSED`      | The requester, agents, or automatically after 7 days                       | —                                                    |
| `RESOLVED`                           | reopen  | `IN_PROGRESS` | The requester, agents                                                      | —                                                    |
| `CLOSED`                             | reopen  | `IN_PROGRESS` | The requester, agents                                                      | Within 30 days of closing                            |
| `NEW`                                | cancel  | `CANCELLED`   | The requester, agents                                                      | —                                                    |
| `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `PENDING` | cancel  | `CANCELLED`   | Agents                                                                     | A reason                                             |

Some moves are refused on purpose. A pending ticket must be resumed before it is resolved. A resolved or closed ticket cannot be cancelled. Nothing leaves `CANCELLED`: if a ticket was cancelled by mistake, open a new one and link it to the old one.

### Problems

| From                         | Action     | To            | Who                                                 | Requires                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `NEW`                        | assign     | `ASSIGNED`    | Agents                                              | —                                                     |
| `NEW`, `ASSIGNED`            | start      | `IN_PROGRESS` | The assignee, any agent if it is unassigned, admins | Starting an unassigned problem makes you its assignee |
| `IN_PROGRESS`                | workaround | `KNOWN_ERROR` | The assignee, admins                                | A documented workaround                               |
| `IN_PROGRESS`, `KNOWN_ERROR` | resolve    | `RESOLVED`    | The assignee, admins                                | A root cause                                          |
| `RESOLVED`                   | close      | `CLOSED`      | Agents, or automatically after 7 days               | —                                                     |
| Any open status              | cancel     | `CANCELLED`   | Agents                                              | A reason                                              |

Problems have no pending state and no SLA clocks.

### Changes

| From                                     | Action   | To                  | Who                                   | Requires                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DRAFT`                                  | submit   | `AWAITING_APPROVAL` | The change's requester, admins        | Planned start and end, risk, implementation plan, rollback plan |
| `AWAITING_APPROVAL`                      | approve  | `APPROVED`          | An eligible approver                  | —                                                               |
| `AWAITING_APPROVAL`                      | reject   | `DRAFT`             | An eligible approver                  | A reason                                                        |
| `APPROVED`                               | start    | `IN_PROGRESS`       | The assignee, admins                  | Confirmation if it is over an hour before the planned start     |
| `IN_PROGRESS`                            | complete | `IMPLEMENTED`       | The assignee, admins                  | Outcome notes                                                   |
| `IN_PROGRESS`                            | rollback | `ROLLED_BACK`       | The assignee, admins                  | Outcome notes                                                   |
| `IMPLEMENTED`, `ROLLED_BACK`             | close    | `CLOSED`            | Agents, or automatically after 7 days | —                                                               |
| `DRAFT`, `AWAITING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` | cancel   | `CANCELLED`         | The change's requester, admins        | —                                                               |

A rejected change goes back to draft to be edited and resubmitted. Its earlier decisions stay on the record.

**Eligible approvers** are admins and owners, and leads of the change's team. In every case the approver must be someone other than the change's requester or assignee. If nobody else is eligible, as in a brand-new workspace with one owner, the owner may approve their own change. Patchgrid labels and audits that as a self-approval. While a change awaits approval, its assignee and team cannot be changed, because they decide who may approve it.

## Editing and conflicts

A requester can edit the title and description of their own ticket while it is `NEW`. Agents can edit the title and description until a ticket is closed or cancelled, and its impact, urgency and category at any time. Changing impact or urgency recomputes the priority.

If two people edit the same ticket at once, the second save is refused and asks you to reload. Nothing is silently overwritten.

## Comments

- **Public comments** are seen by the requester and watchers.
- **Internal notes** are seen only by agents and above. A requester can never read one, even on their own ticket. Email replies are always public.
- You can edit your own comment for 15 minutes. It is then marked as edited, and the earlier text is kept in the ticket's audit trail.
- Admins can remove a comment. Its text is deleted for everyone, the thread shows that a comment was removed, and the removal is audited.

Files can be attached to tickets and comments, up to 10 MB each. Images, PDFs, plain text, CSV, JSON, ZIP, Word and Excel files are accepted. Each file is checked by its content, not its name, and SVG and HTML are refused. A file attached to an internal note is as private as the note.

## Watchers

The requester and the current assignee always receive a ticket's updates. Anyone who can read a ticket can watch it, and agents can add other members as watchers. A requester who watches someone else's ticket sees its public content only: no internal notes, no audit trail. When a ticket is reassigned, the previous assignee stops receiving its updates; they can watch it to keep following it.

## Links

| Link         | Between                                               | Read from the other end |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Caused by    | an incident → a problem                               | "causes"                |
| Resolved by  | a problem → a change                                  | "resolves"              |
| Duplicate of | an incident or service request → one of the same type | "has duplicates"        |
| Relates to   | any two tickets                                       | "relates to"            |

You can raise a problem directly from a set of incidents, which links each of them to it, and raise a change from a problem. Marking a ticket as a duplicate of a duplicate links it to the original instead, so chains never form.
